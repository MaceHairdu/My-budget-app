How to Fill Out the Budget

Input all income deposited into your bank account during the month being used to prepare this budget.

Then input all monthly bills. If a bill is paid in the middle of the month, include it with the next budget preparation after it has actually been paid.

The app subtracts Monthly Bills from Monthly Income to determine your Net. The Net is divided by the actual number of Sunday-through-Saturday budget weeks assigned to the budget month.

Weekly Bills, Needs, and Savings are then subtracted from the Weekly Total. What remains becomes the Fun Money pool.

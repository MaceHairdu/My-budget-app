Paying bills during the month

Assuming today is the first of the month, input all income that was deposited into your bank account during the previous month.

Also input all your monthly bills. If you have a bill that isn’t paid on the 1st of the month, include it as if you paid it on the next ‘1st of the Month’ that occurs.

For example: If you pay your Water Bill on the 16th of every month, you will wait until the 1st of the next month to include it in this budget.

This is so that all income and bills paid throughout the previous month, in addition to the bills paid on the 1st of this month, are used to calculate your budget for next month. This gives you the foresight to plan a month in advance.

What if I don't pay a bill on the 1st?

When preparing your next month's budget, include all income that actually reached your bank account during the previous month.

Include all bills paid during the previous month and any bill that was paid on the first day of the current month.

For example, if you used $100 of electricity during August and paid the bill on September 1st, include that $100 when preparing the October budget.

Bills paid later in September belong to the October budgeting period according to the actual month in which they are paid.
